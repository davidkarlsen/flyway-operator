Metrics are served over HTTPS on port {{ .Values.metrics.port }} by the Service
{{ include "common.names.fullname" . }}-metrics in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.
Requests need a bearer token for a ServiceAccount allowed to GET /metrics. Grant it to your
Prometheus ServiceAccount with:

  kubectl create clusterrolebinding {{ include "common.names.fullname" . }}-metrics-reader \
    --clusterrole={{ include "common.names.fullname" . }}-metrics-reader \
    --serviceaccount=<prometheus-namespace>:<prometheus-serviceaccount>
